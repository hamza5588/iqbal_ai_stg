Understanding PDF Formats

01
Text-based — editable and searchable
02
Scanned — requires OCR
03
Mixed — text and scanned images
1. Text-based Content: This is directly editable and searchable text that can be easily extracted.
2. Scanned Content: These are images of text that have been digitized from physical documents. They require OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to convert the images into machine-readable text.
3. Mixed Content: A PDF may contain both text and scanned images, which can complicate text extraction.
Depending on how the PDF was created, the text extraction process may vary, and some content may not be recognized or retrievable without OCR.
