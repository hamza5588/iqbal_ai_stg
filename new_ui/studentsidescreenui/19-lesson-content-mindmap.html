Understanding PDF Formats

Yes, that's correct. A PDF document may contain different formats of content, including:
01

Text-based Content

This is directly editable and searchable text that can be easily extracted.
PDF Formats
02

Scanned Content

These are images of text that have been digitized from physical documents. They require OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to convert the images into machine-readable text.
03

Mixed Content

A PDF may contain both text and scanned images, which can complicate text extraction.
PDF Formats
i
Depending on how the PDF was created, the text extraction process may vary, and some content may not be recognized or retrievable without OCR.
